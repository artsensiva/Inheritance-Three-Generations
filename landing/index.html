Inheritance: Three Generations

Make a life for yourself. Discover what your children inherit from your choices.

A single-player card-strategy game. Guide one family through three generations of a fictional canal town, and watch an early decision come back as a debt, a locked door or a second chance for someone who has not been born yet.

The demo runs in your browser: no account, no download. It is the first generation of one family and takes roughly 6–8 minutes.

How it works

1

Decide

Seven decisions per generation. Each card gives you two or three different options with visible stakes: security now or opportunity later, schooling or income, land or flexibility, ambition or duty.

2

Inherit

When a generation ends you choose how the estate passes on. Property, debt and duties travel with it. The heir’s health and knowledge are rebuilt from how they were raised, and the game shows you how.

3

Reckon

The ending explains the family’s trajectory using only things that actually happened in your run, and gives you a family tree you can save as an image.

What carries forward

Belongs toWhatAt a generation change
The characterHealth, knowledge, skillsNever copied. The heir’s are built from their upbringing; skills pass down one level lower.
The householdCoin, family bondsCarried over; bonds drift unless nurtured.
The familyProperty, debt, duties, consequencesInherited, divided or sold, depending on how you pass the house on.

A choice that comes back

Choices leave a record, and later events refer to it. For example:

Generation I Master Pell’s school will take your child. Fees are four coin; the workshop bench could use another pair of hands. You sell the workshop to pay.
Generation II The old workshop is a dyer’s shop now. Your heir knows barrels only from the bits of stave kept in a drawer — and the guild remembers the name.

Written from the game’s own events. Sixty events, fourteen delayed-consequence chains, three starting families and four family ambitions in the full game.

An ending worth keeping

Every run ends with a family tree and a legacy card, generated from the choices that were actually made. Save it as an image if you like.
